# Interview Readiness Guide – AI Infrastructure MLOps Engineer

## Purpose
Help you frame the MLOps curriculum into interview-ready narratives focused on pipeline reliability, automation, and collaboration.

## Core Competencies to Highlight
- End-to-end ML pipeline design with quality gates and staging  
- CI/CD for models and data artefacts  
- Monitoring, SLOs, and rollback strategies for ML services  
- Cross-functional alignment with data scientists, platform, and product teams

## Preparation Checklist
1. Pick two pipeline case studies (training + serving) and one incident resolution story.  
2. Assemble artefacts: pipeline diagrams, configs, dashboards, postmortems.  
3. Practice articulating governance decisions and risk mitigation.  
4. Prepare metrics (deployment frequency, failure rate, detection time).

## Mock Interview Structure (50 minutes)
| Segment | Objective | Repository Tie-In |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Background + role focus | Root README |
| 15 min | Pipeline deep dive | Blueprint modules |
| 15 min | Reliability / incident scenario | SLO labs, runbooks |
| 10 min | Collaboration & product impact | Communication templates |
| 5 min | Candidate questions | Below |

## Sample Technical Prompts
- “Describe the CI/CD system you use to promote models from staging to production.”  
- “How do you detect and respond to data or model drift?”  
- “Explain how you manage rollback and auditability for ML deployments.”

## Behavioral Prompts
- Negotiating scope with stakeholders  
- Responding to regression after a model update  
- Mentoring teams that are new to MLOps practices

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer
- “How do your data science and engineering teams collaborate during releases?”  
- “What metrics define success for MLOps initiatives here?”  
- “Which parts of the pipeline are automated vs manual today?”
